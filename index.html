About us

Comfort & Craft is a family-owned furniture workshop founded in 2012. We specialize in beds, chairs, TV stands, and cupboards of all kinds — from rustic to contemporary.

Every piece is made from sustainably sourced hardwood and finished with non-toxic, child-safe materials. Our small team of artisans combines traditional joinery with modern design.

We believe furniture should be both beautiful and functional. That’s why we offer custom dimensions, fabric choices, and finishes to match your space perfectly.
